Real-time direct messages

Photo and video sharing

Frequently asked about NepaChat

What is NepaChat?

NepaChat is a browser-based chat and calling app for direct messages, shared media, a friends Activity feed, and one-to-one calls.

Can I use NepaChat on my phone?

Yes. NepaChat is a web app that can be opened in a supported mobile browser. Audio and video calls need camera or microphone permission.

Can I sign in on more than one device?

Yes. Sign in with the same account on another device to access your server-synced chats.
